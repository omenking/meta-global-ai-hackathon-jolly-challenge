## Lessons Learned
- collecting orbs opens the gate to the warp tile
- the warp tile sends the player to the next level
- the player has basic ability to move around
- when a player moves an orb they pick it up.
  - the orb counter goes down
- the player cannot walk through walls

## Level Concept

A very small level, the player collects three orbs.
While on the path to the gate, the player has to snake
around a wall, since they can't pass through them.

The whole level is in view, as we aren't introducing camera movement yet.

The level is dead simple. It doesn't have fit our fun rubric since its the first level.
